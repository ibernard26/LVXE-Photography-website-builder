## What LVXE is

LVXE is the independent photography and visual-arts practice of Isaiah Bernard
(New Jersey / New York): editorial fashion, intimate portraiture, cinematic
storytelling and fine art. The site should feel like **an interactive luxury
fashion publication staged as a cinematic photography exhibition**, not a
portfolio template.

**Success test:** within the first five seconds a visitor should remember a
distinctive photographic artist, not the website template.

## The intended aesthetic (judge against this)

1. **Neo-Swiss editorial typography:** an oversized grotesk (Geist) for the
   wordmark and headlines, an italic serif (Newsreader) as a counter-voice on
   alternating lines, and small uppercase mono (Geist Mono) for metadata. At
   most four type levels per screen.
2. **Cinematic neo-noir:** dark rooms (Obsidian), restrained light, film grain,
   letterboxed "cinema" bands.
3. **Gallery layouts:** asymmetric, magazine-like placements; light "gallery
   wall" sections (Bone) alternating with dark ones; full-width bands and
   smaller pairs.
4. **Selective experimental motion:**
   - masked image reveals, staggered line reveals, a full-screen menu,
     shared-element page transitions
   - an optional WebGL "darkroom light" over the hero
   - durations of 300–800 ms
   - motion supports the photographs and never competes with them
5. **Graphic system:**
   - issue numbering ("Issue 01")
   - plate (Pl. 01) and series (S—01) catalogue numbers
   - hairline rules and vertical margin labels
   - burgundy used only sparingly: booking links, active states, one period
     mark

**Colour tokens:** Obsidian #0B0B0D · Charcoal #1B1B1F · Bone #F2EFE9 ·
Gray #A7A4A0 · Burgundy #54252D.

## Non-negotiable rules (flag any violation)

- **No AI/SaaS clichés:** no neon glows, cyan/purple gradients, pill badges,
  glassy blur panels, scroll-jacking, custom cursor rings, particle effects,
  or decorative icons/emoji in navigation, buttons or metadata.
- **Image integrity:** photographs are never cropped, stretched or forced into
  uniform shapes. Each shows at its native ratio, with no rounded corners or
  card chrome. A portrait must never be cropped into a horizontal hero.
- **No heavy dark overlays** that obscure the photography.
- **Authentic work only:** no AI-generated or stock imagery, and no invented
  projects, clients, awards, publications, quotes or credits.
- **Commercial separation:** the homepage leads with artistic work, not
  prices. Rates appear only on Commissions. Commercial and corporate clients have
  their own clearly separated path, which must still be easy to find.
- **Accessibility and performance:**
  - keyboard-usable menu (Esc closes it, focus is managed)
  - visible focus states and AA text contrast
  - the reduced-motion setting is respected (no autoplay, no animations)
  - no layout shift while images load
  - a pause control on every video
  - fast first load

## Please assess and score (1–5) with specific evidence

1. First five seconds: does it read as a distinctive photographer, or as a template?
2. Typography: Neo-Swiss + editorial serif, hierarchy, restraint.
3. Photography presentation: native ratios, no cropping, overlay weight, image prominence.
4. Layout rhythm: asymmetry, dark/light alternation, variety without chaos.
5. Motion: purposeful, cinematic, 300–800 ms, not distracting.
6. Graphic system: issue numbers, rules, labels, burgundy discipline.
7. Rule compliance: list every violation of the rules above, with location.
8. Booking experience for corporate and commercial clients.
9. Accessibility and mobile.
10. The three highest-impact changes you would make next.

## Known open items (not defects; confirm only)

- **The booking form isn't connected to a form service yet.** It tells visitors
  nothing was sent.
- **Content to confirm:** series names, rates and concept lines are drafts taken
  from a vision board, pending Isaiah's confirmation.
- **Photos aren't assigned to series yet,** so each photo has its own page.
  Titles read "Untitled" until supplied.
- **Some photos are under 2000 px wide;** higher-resolution exports are pending.
- **The preview flattens page addresses** (e.g. `work-cathedral.html`), and the
  `?type=commercial` booking preset does not survive in the preview host.
