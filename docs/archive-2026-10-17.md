## Titles (17 Oct 2026)

At the owner's request every plate carries a title in place of "Untitled",
written from what the frame shows (light, gesture, setting): no names,
places or events are invented. Any can be renamed in `src/data/plates.ts`.
Film 01 is titled *Chrome Blink*.

Corrections made while titling, after checking each description against
the full-size file: Pl. "Still Life, Rush Hour" shows a masked sitter, so
its description was corrected and it moved to Masks; "Concrete Pact" (was
`seafront-pair`) shows a concrete barrier, not a seafront; "Live Wire" (was
`rooftop-leap`) is a leap above a street. One description that assumed a
sitter's gender was made neutral.
